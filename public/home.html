Complete Yard & Lawn Care

Services Tailored for Mississippi Properties

From weekly precision cutting to pine straw, gutter cleaning, and pond bank reclamation, Luke handles all aspects of property care.

Lawn Mowing & Precision Edging

Consistent, sharp cuts at the proper turf height for healthy Mississippi grass. Includes weed eating along fences and concrete edging for clean curb lines, followed by a clean blower finish.

  • Sharp blades that don't tear grass
  • Sidewalk & driveway edging
  • Full grass clipping blow-off

Pine Straw & Mulch Spreading

Clean flower bed weeding, trench edging, and fresh delivery & installation of long-leaf pine straw or dark hardwood mulch to retain soil moisture and elevate curb appeal.

  • Clean pine straw and mulch delivery
  • Flower bed weed removal
  • Crisp trench border definition

Bush, Hedge & Small Tree Trimming

Keep ornamental hedges sculpted, healthy, and away from siding, roofs, and walking paths. We trim, shape, and thoroughly haul away all trimmings and clippings.

  • Hedge shaping & deadwooding
  • Small ornamental tree maintenance
  • Total branch & debris haul-away

Gutter Cleaning & Flushing

Prevent water overflow and foundation damage with thorough gutter clearance. We scoop out trapped pine needles, leaves, and sludge, ensuring downspouts drain freely.

  • Hand-cleared pine needles & leaves
  • Downspout flow check & flushing
  • Grounds cleanup around house

Pond Perimeter & Overgrowth Clearing

Tackling tough brush, overgrown pond banks, neglected ditch lines, and thick property perimeters that standard lawn mowers can't reach.

  • Pond bank weed cutting
  • Fence line overgrowth reclamation
  • Heavy brush & briar removal

Yard Debris & Junk Cleanouts

Post-storm fallen limbs, dead shrub extraction, leaf carpets, and general yard clutter removal to leave your outdoor property immaculate.

  • Post-storm limb & branch cleanup
  • Move-in / move-out yard refresh
  • Complete off-site disposal
